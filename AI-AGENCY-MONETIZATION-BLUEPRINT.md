## BRANDING STRATEGY: Become the "AI Guy"

### Your Personal Brand Positioning
**One-liner:** "I help businesses replace $4,000/mo employees with $1,500/mo AI systems."

This is your hook. Use it everywhere — bio, intro on calls, first line of every piece of content.

### Brand Identity Pillars
1. **Relatable** — You're not a tech bro in Silicon Valley. You're a real person who figured out AI and now helps regular businesses use it. Show your face, your workspace, your journey.
2. **Proof-driven** — Every post should include a number, a result, or a demo. "47 calls answered." "$12,000 saved." "3 hours automated." Numbers sell.
3. **Niche authority** — Don't be "AI for everyone." Be "AI for contractors and law firms." Specificity = trust.
4. **Accessible** — You explain AI like a friend, not a textbook. No jargon. "We built a robot that answers your phone" beats "We deployed a conversational AI agent."

### Content Brand Voice
- Talk like you're texting a smart friend who owns a business
- Use short sentences. Be direct.
- Lead with the result, not the process: "This automation saved 14 hours/week" not "We integrated Vapi with GoHighLevel via Make.com"
- Use "you" more than "we" or "I"

### Visual Brand
- Color palette: Dark navy + electric blue + white (tech but approachable)
- Always show real dashboards, real automations, real metrics in posts
- Face in every Reel thumbnail — personal brands outperform logos on Instagram
- Consistent template for carousels (use Canva — pick 1 template, stick with it)

## OUTREACH BLUEPRINT: Getting Clients Without Ads

### Channel 1: Instagram DM Outreach (Free, Do This NOW)
**Target: 20 DMs/day, 5 days/week = 100 conversations/week**

**Who to DM:**
- Business owners who follow you (start with your 8K)
- Contractors, HVAC companies, lawyers, and creators who post actively
- People who engage with business/entrepreneurship content

**DM Script (Cold):**
```
Hey [Name]! I came across your page — love what you're
doing with [specific thing about their business].

Quick question: do you ever feel like you're losing leads
because you can't get back to people fast enough?

I help [contractors/lawyers/creators] set up AI systems
that handle calls, follow-ups, and bookings 24/7.
Just built one for a law firm that went from missing
12 calls/week to capturing every single one.

Would you be open to a quick 10-min chat to see if
something like that could work for [their business]?
No pressure either way.
```

**DM Script (Warm — they already follow you or engaged):**
```
Hey [Name]! Thanks for following along.
Noticed you're in [industry] — what's the most
time-consuming part of running your business right now?

I've been building AI automation systems for
[industry] owners and I'm curious if any of the
stuff I post has been relevant to you.
```

**Rules:**
- Personalize EVERY message (mention their business, a recent post, their location)
- Never pitch in the first message — ask a question
- Follow up after 48 hours if no response (once only)
- Track everything in a spreadsheet: Name, Business, DM Sent, Response, Audit Booked, Closed

6. **Your unfair advantage is PROOF** — Mike's project is gold. Film it, screenshot it, talk about it in every piece of content until you have 5 more case studies.
