## Content Pillars (Post 5x/week)

### 1. Before/After Transformations (2x/week)
Show the manual process vs. the automated version.
- "This law firm was losing 12 calls/week. Now AI answers 24/7."
- "This HVAC company booked 40% more estimates with one automation."
- Use split-screen Reels and carousels.

### 2. Education / Value Bombs (2x/week)
Teach your audience what's possible with AI.
- "5 tasks every contractor should automate TODAY"
- "Why your law firm is losing clients while you sleep"
- "The $500/mo system replacing $4,000/mo employees"
- Carousel posts with actionable tips.

### 3. Behind the Scenes / Proof of Work (1x/week)
Show yourself building systems, client wins, dashboards.
- Screen recordings of automations running
- Client testimonial clips
- "Day in the life" building AI systems remotely
