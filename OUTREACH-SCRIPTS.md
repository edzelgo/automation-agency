## RULES FOR OUTREACH

1. **Personalize every single message.** Copy-paste with zero personalization = instant block. Spend 30 seconds looking at their profile/website before sending.
2. **Never pitch in the first message.** Ask a question. Start a conversation. Pitching in DM #1 kills the deal.
3. **Follow up exactly once.** After 48 hours. If they still don't respond, move on. Desperation repels.
4. **Track everything.** If you're not tracking, you're guessing. Use the tracker above.
5. **Send at the right time.** Best DM/email times: Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am or 5–7pm in their timezone.
6. **Lead with their problem, not your solution.** "Are you losing leads?" beats "I sell AI phone systems."
7. **Always have a CTA.** Every message should end with a clear next step: a question, a call link, or an offer to send a video.
