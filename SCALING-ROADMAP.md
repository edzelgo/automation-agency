## PHASE 5: MILLION-DOLLAR RUN (Months 15–20) — $60,000 → $83,333/mo ($1M/year)

### The Math
$1,000,000 / 12 = **$83,333/mo**

To hit this you need ONE of these combinations:
- **24 clients × $3,500/mo average** = $84,000/mo
- **17 clients × $5,000/mo average** = $85,000/mo
- **14 clients × $6,000/mo average** = $84,000/mo

With churn replacement (~10%/mo), you need to close 3–4 new clients/month.

### Actions
1. **Build a small team**
   - You (CEO/visionary): strategy, partnerships, content
   - 2 automation builders: delivery
   - 1 client success manager: retention, upsells
   - 1 setter/closer: sales
   - 2 VAs: admin, content, onboarding
   - **Total team cost: ~$12,000–$15,000/mo**

2. **Diversify lead sources**
   - Paid ads: $5,000/mo → 30+ booked calls/mo
   - Instagram organic: 10+ inbound leads/mo
   - Referrals: 5+ warm leads/mo
   - Partnerships: 5+ leads/mo
   - YouTube (long-form tutorials): 5+ leads/mo

3. **Maximize client lifetime value**
   - Average retention target: 8+ months
   - Upsell/cross-sell every client within 60 days
   - Quarterly business reviews to show ROI and prevent churn
   - Client LTV target: $20,000+

4. **Consider white-labeling**
   - Other agencies resell your automations under their brand
   - You build once, sell many times
   - Additional $10,000–$20,000/mo with minimal extra work

5. **Productize one service**
   - Turn your most popular automation into a DIY SaaS tool
   - $97–$297/mo self-serve product
   - Passive income layer on top of agency revenue
